Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

At Kozy & Klean Nomadic Concierge, we take care of your home, and of your personal information. This policy explains in plain language what we collect on this site, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and how to exercise your rights. It complies with Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (as amended by Law 25) and Canada's federal privacy law (PIPEDA). The French version prevails in case of discrepancy.

2. What we collect and why

Quote request (simulator)

  • Information: name, phone, email, property address, postal code and city, type of residence, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, requested services, frequency, start date and any details you choose to add (e.g. pets, access).
  • Why: to prepare your quote, contact you to finalize it and, if you wish, enter into and carry out the service agreement.
  • How: you enter it yourself; it is emailed to us when you click “Send my quote request”, with your authorization (checkbox).

Quick message

  • Name, phone or email and your message, only to reply to you.

Service area check

  • The city you type to check your area is processed only in your browser. It is not sent to us.

Audience measurement, only if you accept

If you click “Accept” in the banner, we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used and improve it: pages viewed, visit length, scrolling, clicks and pointer movements, device, browser and operating system type, approximate region (derived from your IP address), referring site and a random identifier stored in a cookie. Clarity produces statistics, heatmaps and session replays.

  • Form field contents and the areas that display your contact details (simulator, summary, quick message, confirmation) are masked: Clarity never captures them.
  • We do not try to identify you personally with this data and we do not use it for advertising.
  • If you decline, Clarity is not loaded at all.

Technical data

As with any website, your browser sends your IP address and technical information (browser, date and time) to our host and to the services that provide the site's fonts and email library. This data is used to run and secure the site.

4. Who we share your information with

We never sell, rent or trade your information. We entrust it only to the providers that allow us to offer this site and our services, and only for those purposes:

  • Netlify, Inc. (United States): website hosting.
  • EmailJS Pte. Ltd. (Amazon Web Services servers in the United States): delivers your requests by email. According to its policy, EmailJS does not store message content.
  • Google LLC: the Gmail inbox where we receive and keep your requests; Google Fonts, which provides the site's fonts.
  • jsDelivr (content delivery network): provides the EmailJS technical library.
  • Microsoft Corporation: Microsoft Clarity, only if you accept.

We may also disclose information where required by law, for example at the request of a competent authority.

5. Information stored outside Québec

The providers above may store or process your information outside Québec, mainly in the United States, where it is subject to local laws. Before entrusting information to a provider outside Québec, we assess whether it will receive adequate protection (privacy impact assessment), taking into account its sensitivity, the purposes of its use, the provider's safeguards (encryption, contractual commitments) and the applicable legal framework.

6. Retention

  • Quote requests and messages: as long as needed to handle your request. If no contract is signed, they are deleted no later than 12 months after our last exchange. If you become a client, they are kept during our business relationship, then for the period required by law (including tax law).
  • Microsoft Clarity: session replays kept 30 days; statistics and heatmaps, 9 months (periods set by Microsoft).
  • Your cookie choices: 12 months (6 months if declined), after which we ask again.

At the end of these periods, information is securely destroyed or anonymized.

7. Security

  • The site is served over HTTPS only (encrypted connection).
  • Only the people at Kozy & Klean who need your information to serve you can access it.
  • Our accounts (email, hosting, tools) are protected by strong passwords and two-factor authentication.
  • If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we notify the Commission d'accès à l'information and the people concerned. We keep a register of incidents.

8. Your rights

At any time, you may:

  • find out whether we hold information about you and get a copy (access);
  • have inaccurate, incomplete or ambiguous information corrected (correction);
  • withdraw your consent (for cookies: “Cookie settings” button);
  • ask that information no longer needed be deleted;
  • receive the information you provided in a structured, commonly used technological format (portability).

Write to the person in charge (section 1). We will reply in writing within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. Access is free; if reasonable reproduction fees permitted by law applied, we would tell you in advance.

9. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your information, please write to the person in charge first: we will review your complaint and reply in writing within 30 days. You may also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (1 888 528-7741).

10. Our governance rules

In short, our internal practices provide for:

  • a designated person in charge (section 1) who approves our practices and handles requests;
  • collecting only what is necessary, for specific purposes announced in advance;
  • retention and destruction rules (section 6);
  • a privacy impact assessment before adopting a new tool or transferring information outside Québec;
  • a complaint-handling process (section 9) and a register of confidentiality incidents.

11. Minors

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information about children under 14 without the consent of a parent or guardian.

12. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the current version. If a change affects what you consented to (for example a new measurement tool), we will ask for your choice again.
